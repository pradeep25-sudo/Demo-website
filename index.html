About Apex Academy

Where Conceptual Clarity Meets Unstoppable Results

Founded in 2011 by a passionate team of IIT and Delhi University educators, Apex Academy was born from a simple mission: to replace rote memorization with deep conceptual understanding.

Unlike massive commercial factories where students get lost in crowded halls of 150+ students, Apex Academy maintains an unwavering commitment to small batches (maximum 25 students). We maintain a strict maximum limit of 22 to 25 students per batch. Unlike large corporate coaching institutes where 120+ students sit in lecture halls, our micro-batch structure guarantees that every student receives individualized attention and direct teacher interaction.

Yes! We conduct the Apex Talent Hunt Exam (ATHE) every month. Meritorious students can earn fee scholarships up to 90% based on their test performance. Additionally, special scholarships are awarded to school toppers and NTSE/Olympiad rank holders.

Every single lecture delivered in our classrooms is recorded in high definition. Students can re-watch recorded video backup on our portal at their own pace. Furthermore, mentors conduct dedicated one-on-one catch-up sessions to clarify doubts before the student rejoins regular classes.
